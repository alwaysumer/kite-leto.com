Cookies and browser storage

Our sites set no advertising or analytics cookies and use no visitor counters. Your browser's local storage keeps only two settings, and they never leave your device:

  • langChoice — the site language you picked (EN or RU);
  • pushAskedAt — when we last offered notifications, so we ask no more than once every 14 days.

You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Push notifications

After a while on the site we offer to subscribe to notifications. Your browser asks for permission only if you click "Yes, notify me". Nothing is subscribed without your consent.

What we send: good-wind forecasts for lessons, school news, free spots and course offers.

Notifications are sent through SendPulse. To do this it stores your browser's technical subscription address, browser and device type, language, approximate location (country and city from your IP address) and the subscription date. We do not receive your name, phone or email when you subscribe.

How to unsubscribe: click the lock or settings icon next to the site address and turn off "Notifications", or remove the site under notifications in your browser settings.

On iPhone and iPad, website push notifications work only for sites added to the Home Screen.

When you message us

If you write to us on WhatsApp, Telegram or by email, we see your name and contact in that app. We use them only to reply and arrange lessons, and never pass them on.

Links to other sites

Our sites link to Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Maps and Yandex Maps. When you follow those links, their own rules apply.

Does the site use cookies?

No advertising or analytics cookies. The browser keeps only your language choice and when we last offered notifications.

Click the lock or settings icon next to the site address and turn off "Notifications".

Who sends the notifications?

Always Summer school, through the SendPulse service.
