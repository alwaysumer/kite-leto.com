How much does it cost to learn kitesurfing in Vietnam?

Three formats with a personal instructor — from a single taster session to a full course after which you ride on your own.

Intro lesson — €120 Basic course — €350 Full course — €500

The price depends on the number of hours. Every course includes a personal instructor and all equipment, so there is nothing extra to buy or rent.

Course prices

Intro lesson · 2 hours, 1 day€120
Basic course · 6 hours, 3 days€350
Full course · 10 hours, 5 days€500

Prices are per person. Sessions last 2 hours, one session a day.

What's included

  • A personal instructor for the whole course. Our instructors speak English and Russian.
  • All equipment: kite, board, harness, helmet and impact vest.
  • An IKO certificate after the course, so you can rent kites anywhere in the world.

Which course to choose

  • Intro lesson — if you want to try it and see whether you like it. You learn safety, how to launch a kite and how to steer it.
  • Basic course — adds body dragging, self-rescue and your first water starts.
  • Full course — most students finish it riding in both directions, controlling their speed and choosing their own kite size. The best choice if you want to ride on your own.

More on how the lessons work in our guide “Kitesurfing for beginners”.

Planning your budget

To finish a full course without rushing, add a couple of spare days to your trip: on the rare days without wind the session moves to another day. Come in season to lose fewer days — see “Kitesurfing season in Vietnam by month”.

New Year is the busiest time. If you travel then, book your lessons in advance.
