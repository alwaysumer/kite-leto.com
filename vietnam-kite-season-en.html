Kitesurfing season in Vietnam by month

You can kitesurf in Vietnam almost all year — you just need the right spot. Mui Ne and Phan Rang work in winter, Nha Trang in summer.

November – April: Mui Ne & Phan Rang May – September: Nha Trang April & October: transition

In winter the north-east monsoon blows — that is the season for Mui Ne and Phan Rang. In summer the monsoon fades and a soft thermal wind starts in Nha Trang. That makes Vietnam an almost year-round kite destination.

November – March: Mui Ne & Phan Rang

The main kitesurfing season in Vietnam. The north-east monsoon brings wind almost every day.

  • Mui Ne — season November to March, average wind 16–23 knots. The most reliable months are January to March: steady wind and dry weather.
  • Phan Rang — season November to April, average wind 17–29 knots, usually stronger than in Mui Ne. The flat lagoon water helps you get up on the board faster.

New Year is the busiest time — book your lessons in advance. Early December is the quietest.

April and October

Transition months: the wind is unpredictable. In April you can still catch wind in Phan Rang, but for a kite-only trip it is better to plan for the main season.

May – September: Nha Trang

The winter monsoon fades and a thermal wind starts along the coast: the land heats up during the day and a steady sea breeze comes in. In Nha Trang the average wind is then 12–16 knots — soft and comfortable for beginners, and great for hydrofoiling.

FAQ

When is the best time to kitesurf in Vietnam?

November to April in Mui Ne and Phan Rang, May to September in Nha Trang.

Which months are windiest in Mui Ne?

The most reliable are January to March.

Can you kitesurf in Vietnam in summer?

Yes — from May to September Nha Trang has a 12–16 knot thermal wind.

What is the wind like in Phan Rang?

In season it averages 17–29 knots, usually stronger than in Mui Ne.
